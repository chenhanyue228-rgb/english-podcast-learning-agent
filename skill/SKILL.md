# English Podcast Learning Agent Skill


## Purpose


Convert English podcasts into structured English learning materials.


---

# Input


Supported:

- Youtube URL
- Podcast URL
- Audio file


---

# Workflow


## Step 1

Extract audio


## Step 2

Generate transcript


## Step 3

Analyze English learning content


Must extract:


- Native expressions
- Business vocabulary
- Industry terminology
- Sentence patterns


## Step 4

Highlight transcript


Rules:


Native expressions:

green


Business expressions:

blue


Industry terminology:

yellow



## Step 5

Create Notion document


---

# Output


Notion Learning Page


Structure:


Summary

↓

Transcript

↓

Highlighted Expressions

↓

Vocabulary

↓

Sentence Patterns



---

# Weekly Process


Every week:


Collect completed podcasts


Analyze learning progress


Generate weekly review



---

# Quality Rules


Do not only translate.


Always explain:


- Meaning
- Context
- Usage
- Example sentence


Focus on real-world English usage.
